OUR STORY

Solving the gaps
no one else saw.

Two decades of building enterprise technology taught one lesson above the rest: the biggest gaps in a business are rarely where anyone's looking. Smart Epsilon started with one of those gaps — a product leaves the factory, and from that moment on, nobody can prove where it's been, whether it's genuine, or what actually happened to it along the way.

Track & Trace and Anti-Counterfeiting were built to close that gap directly — giving every unit a verified identity, and giving that identity a way to be checked. The results followed quickly: 320M+ units serialized, 99.2% verified authentic, and a live deployment spanning 10+ countries for a single agrochemical manufacturer alone.

As manufacturers started asking that same identity to do more, the platform grew alongside them — not into a new direction, but into the next problem a client actually had. AI Video Intelligence turned the cameras already watching a warehouse into supply-chain-linked intelligence. Payment Linked Incentives gave distributor payouts the same verified foundation as everything else on the platform — calculated from what was actually collected, not what was claimed.

Today, Smart Epsilon supports manufacturers across multiple industries with 20–25% reductions in stockouts among the outcomes those deployments have already delivered.
